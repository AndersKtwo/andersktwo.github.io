JavaScript Voronoi Playwright

Star Citizen Political Map

A political map of the Star Citizen universe, drawn the way grand-strategy games draw galaxies — faction territories, hyperlane-style jump routes, and a route planner that respects ship size. One self-contained HTML file, no backend.

  • Double-click any of the 90 systems to open an orbital diagram of every planet, moon, station, and jump point on record — about 860 objects total
  • No border is hand-drawn: an ownership resolver decides who owns each system, then a Voronoi pass assigns every point in space to its nearest seed
  • Jittered grid of "void" seeds keeps deep space neutral; lane seeds make territory follow the jump network instead of blobbing
  • Cohesion handling: unclaimed systems get a weighted neutral pocket, and lane seeds are capped at a fixed reach from their endpoint
  • The engine has its own cohesion tests, and a Playwright script opens all 90 system views headlessly and fails the build on any page error
